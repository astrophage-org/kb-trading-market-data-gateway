# Trade Settlement System (`astrophage/trade-settlement-system`)

The **Trade Settlement System** is a critical downstream post-trade clearing and settlement service within the **Global Financial Markets Group (GFMG) Nexus Trading Exchange (NTE)** platform. 

While the [[summaries/market-data-gateway-overview|Market Data Gateway (MDG)]] is responsible for real-time public market data egress, the Trade Settlement System operates in parallel to handle post-trade execution lifecycle operations, participant balance updates, clearinghouse reconciliation, and end-of-day (EOD) settlement.

---

## 1. System Role & Architecture

Within the [[concepts/data-lifecycle|end-to-end data lifecycle]], trade matches generated by the [[entities/order-matching-engine|Order Matching Engine]] are emitted to the [[entities/kafka-broker|Kafka Broker]] on the `nte.trades.matched` topic. 

The Trade Settlement System subscribes to these execution records to facilitate clearing and settlement workflows independently of public market data distribution.

```
                  +-----------------------------------+
                  |   [[entities/order-matching-engine|Order Matching Engine]]   |
                  +-----------------------------------+
                                    |
                                    | (nte.trades.matched)
                                    v
                  +-----------------------------------+
                  |        [[entities/kafka-broker|Kafka Broker]]             |
                  +-----------------------------------+
                                    |
                   +----------------+----------------+
                   |                                 |
                   v                                 v
+-----------------------------------+  +-----------------------------------+
|  [[entities/trade-settlement-system|Trade Settlement System]]   |  |   [[entities/trade-consumer|TradeConsumer]] (MDG)          |
|    (grp: settlement-clearing-grp) |  |    (grp: mdg-trade-group)         |
+-----------------------------------+  +-----------------------------------+
                   |                                 |
                   v                                 v
+-----------------------------------+  +-----------------------------------+
|   Clearinghouses, Depository,     |  |     [[entities/client-manager|ClientManager]] / WebSocket   |
|   Participant Balance Ledgers     |  |         External Clients          |
+-----------------------------------+  +-----------------------------------+
```

By leveraging [[decisions/independent-consumer-groups|independent consumer groups]], the Trade Settlement System processes trade batches at transactional consistency boundaries without introducing latency or coupling to MDG's real-time [[concepts/websocket-broadcasting|WebSocket broadcasting tier]] or [[concepts/ingestion-egress-decoupling|ingestion-egress decoupling pipelines]].

---

## ## Responsibilities

* **Post-Trade Clearing**: Ingesting confirmed trade matches from the `nte.trades.matched` stream and initiating clearance workflows.
* **Trade Reconciliation & Allocation**: Matching trade executions against buyer/seller account portfolios, custody ledgers, and clearing member allocations.
* **Depository & Clearinghouse Interfaces**: Interfacing with external central clearing counterparties (CCPs), custodian banks, and settlement networks.
* **End-of-Day (EOD) Reference Data**: Supplying settlement prices, official closing marks, and end-of-day trade reference data back to exchange services and operational databases.
* **Ledger Settlement & Balancing**: Updating participant cash and asset balances following T+0/T+1 settlement schedules.

---

## ## Dependencies

* **[[entities/order-matching-engine|Order Matching Engine]]**: Upstream generator of canonical trade execution records.
* **[[entities/kafka-broker|Kafka Broker]]**: Event bus infrastructure transporting the `nte.trades.matched` topic.
* **[[entities/protobuf-contracts|Protobuf Wire Contracts]]**: Consumes the `nte.marketdata.TradeTick` message schema containing trade identifiers, timestamps, prices, quantities, and side designations.
* **[[entities/runtime-models|Runtime Models]]**: Aligns with exchange data representations such as `TradeEvent`.
* **[[entities/compliance-surveillance-monitor|Compliance Surveillance Monitor]]**: Sister service monitoring settlement anomalies, market abuse, and clearing integrity.

---

## 2. Integration with Market Data Gateway (MDG)

The Trade Settlement System interacts with the Market Data Gateway ecosystem primarily across two vectors:

1. **Shared Stream Consumption**: Both MDG's [[entities/trade-consumer|TradeConsumer]] and the Settlement System process `nte.trades.matched`. While MDG prioritizes ultra-low-latency fan-out via [[decisions/low-latency-egress-design|low-latency egress mechanisms]], the settlement system guarantees durable persistence and transactional ACID compliance.
2. **Reference Data Provisioning**: The Trade Settlement System supplies official settlement reference data used across the exchange, aiding in baseline pricing, corporate action adjustments, and next-day [[entities/redis-cache|Redis state cache]] hydration parameters (see [[concepts/state-hydration|State Hydration]] and [[decisions/redis-l2-state-caching|Redis L2 State Caching]]).
